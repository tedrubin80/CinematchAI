Getting Started with Cinematch

🎬 How to Use Cinematch
Cinematch is your AI-powered movie recommendation assistant. Simply type your movie preferences, mood, or ask for recommendations in natural language.
Example queries:
"I want a funny movie for tonight"
"Recommend horror movies like The Conjuring"
"What's a good sci-fi movie from the 90s?"
🤖 AI Parameters
Adjust AI parameters to fine-tune your recommendations:
Temperature (0.0-1.0): Controls creativity. Lower = more focused, Higher = more creative
Max Tokens (50-500): Controls response length
Top P (0.0-1.0): Controls word selection diversity
Top K (1-100): Limits vocabulary choices
🛡️ Safety Features
Cinematch includes built-in safety features to ensure age-appropriate recommendations and content filtering for a safe movie discovery experience.
