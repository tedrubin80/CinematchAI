What is Cinematch?

Cinematch is an advanced AI-powered movie recommendation system that helps you discover the perfect film for any moment. Using cutting-edge natural language processing and psychographic profiling, we analyze your preferences, mood, and viewing context to deliver personalized movie suggestions.

How It Works

  • AI Chat Interface: Have natural conversations about movies with our intelligent chatbot
  • Psychographic Survey: Take a brief survey to build your personalized movie profile
  • Adaptive Learning: The system learns from your feedback to improve recommendations over time
  • Multi-Source Data: We aggregate reviews from Rotten Tomatoes, Letterboxd, Metacritic, IMDB, and Reddit
  • Context-Aware: Recommendations adapt to your current mood, viewing context, and time available

Key Features

  • Natural language movie discovery through AI chat
  • 12-archetype psychographic profiling system
  • Mood-based recommendations (relaxing, energizing, thought-provoking, etc.)
  • Therapeutic viewing options for mental health support
  • Couples mode for shared viewing preferences
  • Real-time movie database with 10,000+ films
  • Detailed movie information including plot, cast, ratings, and streaming availability

Privacy & Data Usage

We take your privacy seriously. Cinematch collects minimal data necessary to provide personalized recommendations:

  • Survey responses to build your movie profile
  • Chat conversations for AI recommendation generation
  • Feedback on recommendations to improve our system

We do not:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Track you across other websites
  • Require personal information beyond what's necessary
  • Share your data with advertisers

Reddit Integration

Cinematch uses Reddit's API to analyze community discussions and sentiment about films. We monitor film-focused subreddits like r/movies, r/TrueFilm, and r/MovieSuggestions to understand how real movie lovers discuss and rate films.

Reddit OAuth Access: Our Reddit integration uses read-only access to public subreddit data. We do not access private messages, post on your behalf, or access any personal Reddit account information.

Technology

Cinematch is built using state-of-the-art AI and machine learning technologies:

  • Advanced language models (Claude, GPT, Gemini) for natural conversation
  • PostgreSQL database with full-text search
  • Real-time web scraping for up-to-date movie data
  • Psychographic profiling based on film theory and psychology research
  • Adaptive learning algorithms that improve with user feedback
